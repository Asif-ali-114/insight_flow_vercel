Business intelligence

From raw records to decisions you can explain

Business intelligence is the habit of looking at reliable numbers regularly. Dashboards give you the live view; analysis reports explain what the numbers mean, what they cannot tell you, and what to check next.

Collecting data is not the same as using it

Raw data

What happened

Raw data tells you which transactions occurred: thousands of rows that nobody reads end to end.

Analysis

What it might mean

Analysis explains trends, performance differences, possible causes and the questions worth investigating next. It supports decisions; it does not make them.

Dashboards

What to watch

A well-designed dashboard lets decision-makers monitor the metrics that matter without repeatedly working through large spreadsheets. Reliable analysis helps you make more informed choices; it does not promise higher profit.

What a professional analysis report includes

Reports help decision-makers communicate findings, compare performance, document assumptions and take informed action. A good one is honest about what the data cannot show.

  1. 1. Executive summary
  2. 2. Business question
  3. 3. Dataset overview
  4. 4. Data quality findings
  5. 5. Methodology
  6. 6. Key performance indicators
  7. 7. Visualizations
  8. 8. Major observations
  9. 9. Limitations and assumptions
  10. 10. Practical recommendations
  11. 11. Suggested next steps
