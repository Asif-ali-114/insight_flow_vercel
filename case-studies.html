Case 1 · sample data

Retail Sales Analysis

Business context
A small retailer with 30 products wants to understand its first six months of sales.
The problem
Monthly totals exist, but nobody has compared quarters, categories or individual products.
Data available
About 1,400 order lines (January–June 2025): date, product, category, region, quantity, discount and unit cost.
Analytical approach
Clean the records, calculate monthly revenue and margin, compare Q1 with Q2, rank categories, and list products whose revenue fell.
Tools used
Python (pandas), Chart.js
Dashboard or report preview

Illustrative findings
  • Illustrative finding: Revenue for the period was … at a gross margin of ….
  • Illustrative finding: Q2 revenue was … versus Q1.
  • Illustrative finding: … was the largest category (… of revenue).
  • Illustrative finding: … products sold less revenue in Q2 than in Q1.
Potential business actions
  • Check stock, pricing and promotions for the products that declined before reacting.
  • Review whether the largest category is also the most profitable.
  • Collect a full year of data to look for seasonality.
Deliverables
  • Cleaned dataset
  • Analysis report (PDF or HTML)
  • Executive dashboard
Limitations
Six months cannot show seasonality, and the data cannot explain why products declined.

Case 2 · sample data

E-commerce Performance Dashboard

Business context
An online store wants a single view of traffic and conversion.
The problem
Traffic reports and order reports are separate, so it is unclear which sources convert.
Data available
Weekly sessions, users and conversions by traffic source and landing page (synthetic).
Analytical approach
Define conversion rate as conversions ÷ sessions, aggregate by source and page, and build filters and a landing-page table.
Tools used
Python (pandas), Chart.js
Dashboard or report preview

Illustrative findings
  • Illustrative finding: The sample recorded … sessions with an overall conversion rate of ….
  • Illustrative finding: … brought the most sessions (… of the total).
  • Illustrative finding: … had the highest conversion rate (…).
Potential business actions
  • Look at whether the highest-converting source can be scaled before shifting budget.
  • Review landing pages with high traffic and low conversion.
Deliverables
  • Interactive dashboard
  • KPI definitions
  • CSV export
Limitations
Users are summed weekly and are not unique people. No live analytics integration is implemented in the sample.

Case 3 · sample data

Data Cleaning and Quality Improvement

Business context
A business merges order exports from two systems before reporting.
The problem
Duplicate rows, mixed date formats and blank fields make totals unreliable.
Data available
An order export of about 250 rows with documented injected problems.
Analytical approach
Write explicit rules, standardize safe formatting, remove only exact duplicates, and flag missing or invalid values for a person to decide.
Tools used
Python (pandas), JavaScript validation rules
Dashboard or report preview

Illustrative findings
  • Illustrative finding: … rows were reviewed; … exact duplicates were removed and … records had formatting standardized.
  • Illustrative finding: … missing values were detected and left blank.
  • Illustrative finding: … records were flagged for manual review.
  • Illustrative finding: Completeness moved from … to … only because duplicates left; gaps were not filled.
Potential business actions
  • Ask the data owner to confirm negative quantities and fill missing prices from source records.
  • Add the validation rules to the monthly import.
Deliverables
  • Cleaned file
  • Quality report
  • Summary of changes
  • Optional script
Limitations
Cleaning cannot recover information that was never recorded.

Case 4 · sample data

Inventory Monitoring

Business context
A shop wants to see stock risks without checking each product.
The problem
Stockouts are noticed when customers ask, and slow products tie up cash.
Data available
A stock snapshot for 30 products with unit cost, 90-day sales and month-end stock history.
Analytical approach
Compute reorder points, days of cover and stock status, and rank products by distance to a stockout.
Tools used
Python (pandas), Chart.js
Dashboard or report preview

Illustrative findings
  • Illustrative finding: … of … products were out of stock and … were at or below their reorder point.
  • Illustrative finding: Stock on hand was valued at … at cost.
  • Illustrative finding: … was the closest product to running out.
Potential business actions
  • Review reorder quantities for out-of-stock products with strong recent sales.
  • Check whether slow movers justify their stock level.
Deliverables
  • Stock status dashboard
  • Reorder list
Limitations
Reorder points rest on a 90-day sales average and assumed lead times; they are not a demand forecast.

Case 5 · sample data

Monthly Financial Reporting

Business context
A small business prepares a monthly income and expense summary by hand.
The problem
The result is slow to produce and budget variances are noticed late.
Data available
Monthly income and expense lines by category with budgets (synthetic).
Analytical approach
Standardize categories, compute income, expenses and a net operating result with stated formulas, and compare with budget.
Tools used
Python (pandas), Excel, Chart.js
Dashboard or report preview

Illustrative findings
  • Illustrative finding: Income was … and recorded expenses …, a net operating result of ….
  • Illustrative finding: The monthly net operating result was negative in … months.
  • Illustrative finding: … was the largest expense category (… of the total); total spend was ….
Potential business actions
  • Discuss the months with a negative result with whoever manages the budget.
  • Review the largest cost category for savings that do not harm service.
Deliverables
  • Monthly financial summary
  • Budget variance table
Limitations
Net operating result is not net profit or cash flow, and this is analytical reporting rather than accounting.

Case 6 · sample data

Student Performance Analysis

Business context
A tutoring center wants to see class-level results across assessments.
The problem
Marks are in separate spreadsheets and reports risk exposing individual students.
Data available
Fictional students with marks and attendance per assessment (pseudonymous IDs, aggregates only).
Analytical approach
Pseudonymize IDs, calculate averages and pass rates by class and subject, and suppress groups under ten results.
Tools used
Python (pandas), Chart.js
Dashboard or report preview

Illustrative findings
  • Illustrative finding: … fictional students were included, with an average mark of … and a pass rate of ….
  • Illustrative finding: Highest subject average: …; lowest: ….
  • Illustrative finding: …. This is an association in sample data, not proof of cause.
Potential business actions
  • Offer extra support in the lowest-scoring subject.
  • Look at attendance patterns before drawing conclusions.
Deliverables
  • Class and subject summary
  • Assessment trend dashboard
Limitations
Small groups are hidden for privacy, and marks do not capture every aspect of learning.
